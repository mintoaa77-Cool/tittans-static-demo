01 — Who we are

Making a difference together

Titans Sports and Social Club is a community-focused organisation dedicated to improving health, wellbeing and quality of life.

We work to address inequalities and barriers affecting individuals and families, particularly within South Asian communities, while creating opportunities for people from all backgrounds to connect, participate and thrive

Our approach recognises that healthier communities are built through more than physical activity. Mental wellbeing, social connection and community participation are equally important.

Mission & Vision.

Our Mission

Build healthier, happier and more connected communities where everyone has the opportunity to participate, achieve their potential and enjoy a better quality of life.

Our Vision

Improve physical, mental and social wellbeing through inclusive sports, social activities, community engagement and development.
