Your privacy

Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Titans Manchester Sports and Social Club is the controller responsible for your personal information. Contact us about anything in this policy:

  • Address: 189 Lawtonmoor Road, Manchester, M23 0PW
  • Email: email@titansmanchester.co.uk
  • Phone: +44 7886 481023

2. Information we collect

  • From you: your name, email address, phone number and message when you use our contact form, email or call us.
  • Photos: photos taken at our sessions, tournaments and events, some of which appear in our photo gallery.
  • Technical information: your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, received by our website hosting and font providers when you visit.

You do not have to give us your details, but if you do not provide your name and a way to contact you, we will not be able to reply to your enquiry.

3. How we use it and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis
Replying to your enquiry and keeping a record of itLegitimate interests: answering questions about the club and its activities
Arranging for you to take part or join, if you ask toSteps you have asked us to take before you join (contract)
Running the website securely and preventing spam or misuseLegitimate interests: keeping our website and data secure
Sharing photos of our activities on this websiteLegitimate interests: showing our community and encouraging participation
Meeting legal obligations and dealing with legal claimsLegal obligation, or legitimate interests

We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google (Sheets and Gmail): stores contact form messages and delivers them to us by email.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the typefaces used on this website.
  • Microsoft Azure and Hostinger: host the website.
  • The police, regulators or other authorities, where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. These transfers are protected by UK adequacy regulations (such as the UK–US data bridge) or by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a member.
  • Technical information: for the periods set by our hosting and font providers.
  • Photos: while they are relevant to our activities, or until you ask us to remove them.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it (including photos of you), to data portability, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Contact us using the details in section 1.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your personal information, contact us first using the details in section 1. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.
