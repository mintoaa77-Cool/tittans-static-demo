Our commitment to communities

Change shaped by the people it serves.

At the heart of Titans Sports and Social Club is a commitment to community engagement and development. We believe that sustainable improvements in health and wellbeing can only be achieved by working alongside communities, understanding their lived experiences, recognising their strengths and responding to their priorities.

We aim to create opportunities for individuals, families and community groups to actively participate in shaping and delivering activities and initiatives that make a meaningful difference.

Our community-focused approach will include:
01

Identifying and addressing health and social inequalities.

02

Promoting community cohesion and building bridges between communities.

03

Addressing barriers experienced by South Asian communities in accessing health, social care, leisure and wider community services.

04

Tackling issues such as social isolation, loneliness, health inequalities, mental health stigma and exclusion.

05

Promoting equality, diversity, inclusion and cultural understanding.

06

Developing partnerships and collaborative approaches to community improvement.

07

Encouraging volunteering, community leadership and active citizenship.

Listen to communities. Recognise their strengths. Build meaningful and lasting change together.
