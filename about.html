About Titans Manchester

Wellbeing.
Belonging.
Better futures.

Titans Manchester Sports and Social Club is a community-focused organisation committed to improving the health, wellbeing and quality of life of individuals, families and communities.

01 — Our purpose

Everyone deserves the chance to thrive.

We have a particular focus on addressing the needs and inequalities experienced within South Asian communities, while promoting an inclusive approach that encourages people from all backgrounds to participate, connect and thrive.

We believe that wellbeing goes beyond physical health. It encompasses our physical, mental and social wellbeing, alongside the opportunities we have to connect, participate, develop and reach our full potential.

03 — Our commitment

Working with communities.

At the heart of Titans Manchester Sports and Social Club is a commitment to community engagement and development.

We believe sustainable improvements in health and wellbeing can only be achieved by working alongside communities, understanding their lived experiences, recognising their strengths and responding to their priorities.

We aim to empower individuals, families a nd community groups to participate in shaping initiatives that make a meaningful and lasting difference.
