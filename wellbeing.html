Healthier people. Stronger communities.

Our approach to wellbeing.

Our approach is underpinned by three distinct but interconnected workstreams – Physical Wellbeing, Mental Wellbeing and Social Wellbeing. Together, these provide a holistic and inclusive approach to improving individual and community wellbeing.

Our Three Key Workstreams
01

Physical Wellbeing

Promoting healthier and more active lifestyles through sport, physical activity, fitness and health promotion.

Our work aims to:

  • Encourage participation in sports, exercise and recreational activities across all ages and abilities.
  • Promote healthy lifestyles, physical activity and preventative health.
  • Raise awareness of health conditions that disproportionately affect South Asian communities, including cardiovascular disease, diabetes and other long-term conditions.
  • Reduce barriers to accessing physical activity and health improvement opportunities.
  • Create inclusive opportunities for individuals and families to become more active and improve their overall health.
02

Mental Wellbeing

Recognising the importance of mental health as an essential part of overall wellbeing, we aim to promote positive mental health, emotional resilience and psychological wellbeing.

Our work aims to:

  • Raise awareness of mental health and challenge stigma, particularly within South Asian communities.
  • Create safe, supportive and culturally sensitive spaces where people feel comfortable seeking help and support.
  • Promote emotional resilience, confidence, self-esteem and positive coping strategies.
  • Encourage social connection and reduce loneliness, isolation and exclusion.
  • Improve awareness of available mental health services and support, helping people access appropriate help at an early stage.
03

Social Wellbeing

Building stronger, more connected and inclusive communities through meaningful participation, community engagement and social development.

Our work aims to:

  • Develop opportunities for social interaction, friendship, participation and community involvement.
  • Promote community cohesion, integration, mutual understanding and respect across different cultures and generations.
  • Reduce social isolation and tackle barriers to participation.
  • Support community-led initiatives, volunteering, leadership and skills development.
  • Strengthen partnerships with local organisations, statutory services and community groups.
  • Empower individuals and communities to influence decisions and contribute positively to local development.
